## Typed settled contract

At `2392732`, `settled=true` is not a boolean carried by a layout callback. A
receipt is consumable only when its activation/input/intent/presentation/tail
identity is current, the DOM presentation revision equals the snapshot
revision, at least one current row is hit-tested in `visibleRowIDs`, and (for
following/cold receipts) the current tail row is hit-tested while the physical
scroll position is at the tail. A stale or fixture-only frame must publish
`settled=false` and retry.

The exact clean-239 browser evidence is recorded in
`audit-output/BROWSER-N-S-FAE8B70-MIGRATION.md`: cached hydration repeated ten
times with current activation, matching DOM/snapshot revisions, non-empty
hit-tested IDs, and no `settled=true && visibleRowIDs=[]` sample. That closes
the cold-following typed paint-fence contract, but it does not by itself prove
the four independent wheel/selection/epoch unit scenarios below.
